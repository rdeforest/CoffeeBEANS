# Intro

It's the late 80s. The Cold War is still ongoing, though not to the degree
that it had been in prior decades. The NATO allies see the USSR as a great
lumbering bear which might be about to lash out or fall over. Espionage is
critically important to maintaining all the illusions required to keep the
public mollified and to be ready to get out of the way of the bear's paw, maw
or carcass, whatever the danger ends up being.

To serve the needs of the allies' espionage agencies, they set up a
multi-national project, BEANS, to locate and train candidate agents. Their
greatest tool is a video game through which the head of BEANS, Agent Toe,
communicates meets, tests and trains future agents.

But partway through the player's training, events transpire which put Agent
Toe in danger. She interrupts the training to solicit the player's assistance
with code breaking, data visualization, operation of remote spying equipment,
and other tasks. Will the player complete all the tasks in time to save Agent
Toe? Only time will tell.
